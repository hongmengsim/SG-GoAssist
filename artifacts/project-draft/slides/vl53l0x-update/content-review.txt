Appendix A. Intended journey 09 Stage Passenger or system action Required outcome 1  Request and recognise Select assistance and bus. Perception adds cues. Explicit intent remains authoritative. 2  Prepare and dock Reserve dwell and align with the boarding fixture. Correct bus, stop and ramp reach. 3  Mark and check Mark area. Check camera and VL53L0X inputs. Clear, fresh safety inputs. 4  Deploy and board Open door, deploy ramp and maintain dwell. Verify deployed position and monitor interlocks. 5  Complete Retract, verify stowed and close the door. Save outcome or escalate a fault. Second demonstration scenario: extra time and guidance without a ramp request.
